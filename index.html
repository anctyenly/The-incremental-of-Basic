How to Play

Buy multiplier upgrades to increase your points per second.

At 10,000 points, you can prestige — reset your points and upgrades in exchange for prestige points (PP). PP give a permanent point multiplier.

At 10,000 total PP, Prestige Challenges unlock. Complete all 4 for powerful rewards and new prestige upgrades.
